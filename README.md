# Pokemon Battle Sim

This application is a terminal-based Pokemon battle simulator using damage calculation from the first generation for simplified Pokemon battles.

## Features
- create a Pokemon with a name, level, attack, defense, hp, moves, and type(s)
- view all Pokemon and their stats, including presets and those you make
- battle with and against four preset Pokemon and any custom Pokemon you make in the session
- calculate the range of damage a move could do to yourself or your opponent
- attack the opposing Pokemon using moves, and see how the battle plays out accordingly
- view the moves and current hp of your Pokemon and your opponent

## Future Functionality
- database functionality to make custom Pokemon persist after the session
- status effects (poisoned, burned, paralyzed, etc.) that certain moves could cause
- non-attacking moves for changing stats, weather, etc.
- priority moves
